How 4.74 days is calculated. The cross-session funnel tracks each of the 71 users individually — it records the exact timestamp when they hit the Complete Registration page (step 1) and the exact timestamp of their first Bid Now Click (step 2), regardless of how many days or sessions passed in between. Fullstory then computes the elapsed duration for each of those 71 users and returns the p50 — the median of all 71 durations. The raw output is 409,178,480 milliseconds: divide by 1,000 → 409,178 seconds → divide by 3,600 → 113.7 hours → divide by 24 → 4.74 days. Median rather than average because the distribution is right-skewed — 32% of first-bidders take 8–90 days, which would inflate a mean. The median represents what a typical first-bidder actually experiences.
